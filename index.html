Unofficial companion app · iPhone, iPad and Android

StajNano makes the Nano Cortex a complete live multi-effects unit — on its own.

Big, high-contrast tiles for the effects, amp and cab, presets, tempo and tuner, and Song mode, which loads a whole song in one touch. Your phone or tablet next to it, an inexpensive Bluetooth MIDI foot controller under your foot, and the Nano Cortex is a complete stage rig.

SONG MODE

A whole song in one touch.

Name a song, set its tempo, choose four presets for 1A, 1B, 2A and 2B and give every slot a name of your own. Load the song and the stage is set: the four presets are on the footswitches, the first one is loaded and the tempo is in place.

  • Songs, not preset numbersThe footswitches say Verse, Chorus, Solo, Outro — the names you chose, in any language, even emoji.
  • One swipe, one songSwipe, or press a foot-pedal button, and the next song loads: its four presets and its tempo together.
  • The tempo comes with itEvery song carries its tempo and sets it for you. Prefer your own tempo? Switch it off in Options.
  • Up to 25 songsKept on your device. Edit them in seconds: pick the presets from the list, type the names, save.
  • Back up, move, shareExport your whole song list to a .stajnano file and import it on another phone or tablet.
  • Made for a foot pedalA Bluetooth MIDI foot pedal steps through your songs and presses the slots. See the MIDI reference.
Song mode belongs to StajNano. It is StajNano’s own feature: it is not built into the Nano Cortex and your songs are not stored on it. They live in StajNano, on your phone or tablet, so Song mode works only while you use the Nano Cortex together with StajNano. When you load a song, StajNano puts its four presets on the footswitches and sets the tempo for you.

Proven on stage with StajPilot

Song mode first proved itself in StajPilot, our stage controller for the Kemper Profiler. StajNano brings it to the Nano Cortex, made for it from the start.

Add an inexpensive foot controller — and the stage is set.

Connect a cheap Bluetooth MIDI foot controller, such as the M-VAVE Chocolate, to StajNano and your feet run the whole show. No cable to the Nano Cortex, no computer, nothing else to carry.

SONGS

Previous / next song

One press loads the next or the previous song: its four presets and its tempo.

SLOTS

The four slots

Press 1A, 1B, 2A or 2B to load that preset, in any mode.

TEMPO

Tap tempo

Tap the tempo on your foot controller and the Nano Cortex follows.

BLOCKS

Gate, effects, amp, cab

Switch any block on or off, or flip it, from your feet.

Setting it up takes a minute: Options → BT Pedal → On, pick your foot controller, set the channel. Every message is in the MIDI reference.

Made for playing live

CONTROL

One screen to play

The gate, five effect slots, amp and cab, the preset name, six amp knobs, the tempo and the four footswitch slots. Tap to switch a block on or off, long-press to edit it. Colours and sizes are chosen to read on a dark stage.

EDIT

Every parameter, one touch away

Open any block to change its model and turn every knob and switch, in the order and with the units you already know. A bar shows the value you are touching, with a slider for fine changes.

PRESET · BANK · SONG

Three ways to move

Preset mode steps through presets. Bank mode puts four presets on the footswitches at once. Song mode loads a whole song: its four presets and its tempo. Swipe left or right below the effects to go to the next or previous one.

TUNER

A tuner you can read from the stage

A big note, a scale with one LED for every cent, and a green glow when you are in tune. It fills the lower part of the control page, or the whole screen if you prefer.

FOOT PEDAL

Bluetooth foot pedals

An inexpensive Bluetooth MIDI foot controller, such as the M-VAVE Chocolate, steps through your songs, presses the four slots, taps the tempo and switches the gate, effects, amp and cab. The MIDI reference lists every message.

AUTO-CONNECT

Connects by itself

Register your pedal once. Next time StajNano finds it and connects without a tap, and it never connects to anybody else’s pedal by itself.

PRIVATE

Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Your songs and settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

The screens

Every page is designed once on a 16:9 canvas and scaled to your screen, so it looks the same on every phone and tablet and the text never follows the system font size.

The screens are drawn by the app itself with sample data.
